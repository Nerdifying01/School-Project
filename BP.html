Description

I am a sentient potato who wears a suit. My full name is Bartholomew Potysi, though most people simply call me British Potato.

Personality

The Potato species is as diverse as humanity. My own personality is defined by charismatic speech, bravery, determination, and the ability to speak so convincingly that I am often compared to a perfect lawyer.

Origin / Creation

Long ago in London, a man was walking home from the market carrying a sack of potatoes — my earliest ancestors.

A violent storm swept across the city and grew into a monstrous hurricane. The man was thrown from his feet and lost his grip on everything he carried… everything except the tightly tied sack of potatoes.

Moments later, lightning struck the sack directly. Whether by fate, miracle, or pure coincidence, every potato inside became alive.

After the storm passed, the newly sentient potatoes wandered the ruined streets of London, learning human language, customs, and etiquette. Eventually their first great leader, Sir Potasius the Dirt, united the scattered communities and led them to a safe haven.

It was in that safe haven that I was grown — from a stem into the distinguished gentleman you see today.

Interview Transcript

The following is a transcript of NDY-E-BP0’s regular century-scheduled interview.

Interviewer: NDY-E-DS2
Interviewee: NDY-E-BP0 — Bartholomew Potysi (“British Potato”)

NDY-E-DS2: Good evening. For the record, could you state your name?

British Potato: Certainly. My full name is Bartholomew Potysi, though most know me simply as British Potato. It’s far less of a mouthful.

NDY-E-DS2: Thank you. Now, many people watching are seeing you for the first time. Could you describe what you are?

British Potato: I am a sentient potato. Contrary to popular belief, we’re neither vegetables with faces nor magical curiosities. We’re an intelligent species with our own history, customs, and society.

NDY-E-DS2: Your appearance has attracted quite a bit of attention.

British Potato: (Adjusts golden monocle.) I should hope so. One ought to make a respectable first impression. I wear black trousers, a black vest over a white button-down shirt, a red tie, and this rather splendid golden monocle. A gentleman’s attire is an extension of his character.

NDY-E-DS2: How would you describe your personality?

British Potato: I’d say I’m charismatic, determined, and brave. I enjoy a good debate, and I have a habit of convincing people rather easily. Some have compared me to an exceptionally talented lawyer. I simply call it knowing how to make a sound argument.

NDY-E-DS2: Let’s discuss your origins. How did your people come into existence?

British Potato: Ah… now there’s a story.
Long ago, in London, a man was walking home from the market carrying his groceries. Among them was a tightly tied sack of potatoes — my earliest ancestors.
A violent storm swept across the city. It grew into a monstrous hurricane that threw the man from his feet, scattering everything he carried. Everything except the sack.
Moments later, lightning struck it directly. Whether by fate, miracle, or sheer coincidence, every potato inside became alive.
The storm claimed countless lives, but my ancestors endured.

NDY-E-DS2: What happened after that?

British Potato: They wandered the city, learning from humanity. They observed British customs, language, etiquette, and governance. Eventually, our first great leader, Sir Potasius the Dirt, united the scattered potato communities and led them to a safe haven where our civilization could flourish. It was there that I was grown — from a stem into the distinguished gentleman you see before you today.

NDY-E-DS2: Your people have leaders?

British Potato: Naturally. Civilization cannot exist without order. We’ve always believed that wisdom and diplomacy should guide a nation before force ever does.

NDY-E-DS2: You’ve become something of a public figure. Does that responsibility weigh on you?

British Potato: Every day. When others look to you for guidance, your words carry weight. That means speaking honestly, remaining calm under pressure, and setting an example worth following.

NDY-E-DS2: Have you ever wished you lived an ordinary life instead?

British Potato: (Chuckles.) An ordinary potato would likely end up on someone’s dinner plate. I believe my circumstances are preferable.

NDY-E-DS2: Fair point. What message would you like to leave to everyone watching?

British Potato: Judge others not by their appearance, but by their actions. Greatness can emerge from the most unlikely places — even from a humble potato. Conduct yourself with dignity, stand by your principles, and never underestimate the power of a well-spoken argument.

NDY-E-DS2: Thank you for your time, Mr. Potysi.

British Potato: The pleasure has been entirely mine.
