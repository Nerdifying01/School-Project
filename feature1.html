Taxation and Economy:

Adjust income Tax Rates

Increase Taxes - We are going to increase income tax for the higher income brackets and decrease tax slightly for those of lower income brackets. From this change we can gain some extra money while averaging the universal income of the population.

Invest in Healthcare and Infrastructure - We want to direct money gained from the slight increase taxes into healthcare and infrastructure so we can urbanise rural areas so that we can make our country stronger all together instead of bringing funding into main areas which makes it hard for us to push the value of our currency as we aren’t fully developed.

Foreign Business Tax - We are going to add a foreign business tax to any overseas business who wants to work in New Zealand. Not only will this decision allow us to make more money, it also allows us New Zealand businesses to thrive so that we can have a bigger image when facing this huge international market.
