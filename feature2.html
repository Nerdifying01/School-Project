Housing:

Land Owner Ship

Build More Schools - We plan to build more schools in places where many houses are present but not educational facilities. When we get all of these schools people can choose to live where they want so they are not restricted by school zones and then prices of housing go up in these areas.

First House Account - This account will be a new service for those who are aged between 18 and 24 years old. For these six years they can put in money towards their first home deposit. This account will have higher interest so that it becomes more enticing to buy a house. You can collect it between the ages 30 and 32.

Lowering Minimum Deposit for Mortgage - When people make a deposit for houses we will lower the minimum deposit required so that it is easier to pay back a mortgage and you don’t have to invest all your money to just get started.

Smaller House Construction - We are going to make houses smaller so that we can have more people in an area so that we don't have to take more land that can be preserved for other uses.

Urbanisation of Rural Areas - Since power is centralized in multiple different areas around New Zealand we are going to make sure that all this urban structure and development is evenly spread throughout New Zealand. In theory we should have at least a place to stay within a 20 kilometer radius of anywhere in New Zealand.
