Education:

Creating a better learning environments

Structured foundation for primary students. Build their foundation as strong as possible. Teachers focus on the basics and spend more time building students up from primary. They can then learn more advanced skills in high school as their foundation would’ve already been built.

Build more schools in urban areas so no one will have to travel very far to get to school and will raise attendance. More students will attend and more schools means smaller classes where the teacher can focus on specific students. Schools do not have to be giant. They only need classrooms and a play to be for recess. People living in a specific school zone pay small donations to fund a small school. Primaries will be focused on as students need foundation, then they can go to high schools which are further spread but offer more opportunities.

Offer more internships and learning opportunities for people becoming teachers and university/highschool students.
