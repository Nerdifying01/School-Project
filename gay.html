What to do

To prepare for possible earthquakes, it is advised that you have the tools necessary to survive in an emergency such as fire extinguishers, first aid kits, flashlights, etc. It is incredibly important to make sure you have these items in your vicinity as an earthquake can lead to other disasters and can leave you stranded without supplies. It is also smart to make sure that your important and expensive belongings are insured. This way, you can get back on your feet quickly and remain in a manageable financial situation as an earthquake will likely destroy many assets. Learning techniques and procedures such as basic first aid and evacuation routes is also crucial preparation for potential earthquakes. First aid is vital as it will allow you to administer life saving treatment to yourself and other injured civilians in case of emergency. Knowing your way around a facility is also necessary as you can exit in the most efficient and safest way possible, guiding others along the way.

During an earthquake, remain calm and stay where you are. If you are outside, stay outside and vice versa. If indoors, it is best to position yourself under sturdy furniture such as desks as that will protect you from falling debris. If no furniture is available, crawl and scrunch your body into a turtle-like position, covering your head with your hands. This is the safest position to be in as it protects all your vital body parts. If outside, relocate to an open area far from power lines or structures as they are likely to fall on you. If the earthquake is particularly strong to the point where you cannot stand, try to move inland as soon as possible. This is important because such powerful earthquakes often trigger tsunamis which are incredibly deadly disasters capable of 100% mortality rates if struck. Do not use anything capable of starting fire such as a match, lighter, etc for light. During an earthquake, it is common for gas lines to break, leaking flammable gases everywhere. If a fire is started during these conditions, the gas will certainly ignite, consuming the whole building in a sea of flames. Make sure to stay away from elevators. Despite the speed and convenience that elevators offer, they can easily shut down or malfunction during an earthquake which could put your life at risk.

After an earthquake, make sure to check yourself and others for any injuries. This is important as there may be many injured, immobilised or trapped people who may not survive until rescue services arrive. Getting to them and ensuring the safety of yourself and them is the number one priority. Stay away from beaches. Tsunamis have a real possibility of occurring so it is crucial to get out of the area before it forms. Tsunamis will engulf anything in its way and no, you cannot outrun it. Check for damage to gas lines, water pipes, electric lines, etc. If they are damaged, make sure to turn them off immediately as they can be incredibly hazardous to any unsuspecting person. If you smell gas, open the windows and get out.
