Reading for Beginners

In the beginning there were five dimensions. The 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th dimensions each contained a finite number of universes. As these universes grew older, the background energy within them increased. This energy is known as Universal Background Radiation, or UBR.

Over millions and millions of supereons, the 5th dimension became so unstable from its universes’ UBR that it literally imploded. The entire 5th dimension was destroyed. Only five evacuées survived. One of them was the Nerdivator.

On that day, the Nerdivator created Nerdify — to Neutralise any unstable universe that might spread too much UBR, Dispose of any extra UBR, and Yield new universes in its place.
